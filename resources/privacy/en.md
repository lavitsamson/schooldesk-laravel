## 3. Who receives data

- Teachers see the classes and subjects they teach. Guardians see their own children only.
- **Payments**: online payments are handled by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland). Card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by the school.
- **Email**: messages are sent through [email provider].
- **AI comment drafts** (only if the school turns them on): to help teachers write report-card comments, a student's first name, subject grades and attendance rate are sent to Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, USA). No surname, date of birth, address or other details are sent. A teacher checks and edits every draft; nothing is published without their approval. Transfers to the USA rely on [the EU–US Data Privacy Framework / standard contractual clauses].
- Hosting: [hosting provider, location].

## 4. How long we keep data

## 5. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data or your child's data and receive a copy (Art. 15, 20), have it corrected (Art. 16), erased (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18), and object to processing (Art. 21). Erasure keeps only what the school must retain by law (for example marks and invoices), in a form that no longer identifies the student.

Contact [email] to use these rights. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example [supervisory authority].
